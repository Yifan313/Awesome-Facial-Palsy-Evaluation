## Facial Palsy Datasets
|Dataset|Year|Samples|Subject|Source|Facial movement distribution|Annotation|Paper|
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|-|-|
|YFP|2018|32 videos|21 patients|YouTub|Random facial movements: at rest, open mouth, close the eyes lightly, elevation of eyebrows, pursing lips, etc.|Palsy facial region (eyes or mouth) in each video|[Hierarchical network for facial palsy detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08971)|
|MEEI|2020|60 videos and 480 imagess|9 healthy subjects and 51 patientss|Lab|8 facial movements: face at rest, eyebrows raised, gentle eye closure, forceful eye closure, gentle smile, forceful smile, pucker, and showing bottom teeth.|Each subject was labelled with an eFACE score|[The spectrum of facial palsy: The MEEI facial palsy photo and video standard set](https://blog.research.google/2019/09/contributing-datato-deepfake-detection.html)|
|AFLFP|2023|5,632 images|88 subjects|Lab|16 facial movements: brow raise, close smile, frown, funny, gentle eye closure, left eyebrow, left smile, left snarl, left wink, open smile, right eyebrow, right smile, right snarl, right wink, snarl, and tight eye closure.|Each image was labelled with 68 facial landmarks|[AFLFP: A database with annotated facial landmarks for facial palsy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.01717|
